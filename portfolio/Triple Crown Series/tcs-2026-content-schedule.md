**Site:** triplecrownseries.ca

## Channel cadence (the simple rules)

- **Social:** 1 post/day minimum during race weeks, 3–4 posts/week off-weeks. Stories daily.
- **Website news:** 1–2 articles per race weekend (preview + recap). 1 article/week off-weeks.
- **Email:** 1 newsletter per week, every Wednesday at 10:00 AM ET. Race-day reminder Saturday 8:00 AM local. Recap Monday 10:00 AM ET on race weeks.
